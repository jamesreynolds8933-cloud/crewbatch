Questions

Before you install

Is my footage uploaded anywhere?

No. CrewBatch runs on your own computer. Clips, previews and captions never leave it. The internet is only used to activate your subscription.

What computer do I need?

A Windows 10 or 11 laptop or PC (64-bit). Any recent laptop handles previews and Shorts. Captions are quicker on a faster processor.

Windows says "Windows protected your PC". Is it safe?

Yes. Windows shows that for new apps from small makers that haven't paid for a certificate yet. Click More info, then Run anyway. You only see it once.

How do I download it?

Click Download, then click CrewBatch-Setup (the .exe file) on the page that opens. Double-click it when it's downloaded and follow the steps. It takes about two minutes.

How do updates work?

Free, and one click. When a new version is out, CrewBatch shows a green Update now button. It updates itself and opens again, with your licence, presets and history untouched.

Does it change my original files?

Never. CrewBatch only reads your footage and writes new files into a separate folder.

What happens after the free trial?

Processing pauses until you subscribe. Your finished videos, presets and analytics stay where they are.

Which languages do captions support?

English and Afrikaans work well, plus Portuguese, French and Swahili. isiZulu and isiXhosa aren't supported by the speech engine yet.

How do I cancel?

Use the cancel link in your Paystack payment email. Your key keeps working until the end of the month you paid for.
